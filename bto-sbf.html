Selling HDB to buy BTO / SBF

Intended for eligible existing owners with an extra transition to coordinate.

  • Projected sale proceeds and CPF accrued interest
  • Resale levy and second housing-loan considerations
  • Sell-before / sell-around / sell-after-key scenarios
  • Cash-flow gaps, interim housing and moving costs
