Privacy & Disclaimer

How this calculator works

The calculator produces indicative property-financing estimates from information you enter. It is not an official HDB, MAS, CPF Board, IRAS or bank service, and it does not determine whether you qualify for a flat, loan, grant or stamp duty relief.

Your personal financial information

The V4.1 calculator processes input values in your browser. The guided journey uses browser session storage to preserve answers while you navigate within the current session. It does not require an account or send your form entries to our website server to produce estimates.

Downloading a PDF generates a copy on your device. If you choose a WhatsApp button, a prepared message containing the selected figures is opened in WhatsApp and is only sent when you confirm sending it. WhatsApp operates under its own privacy terms.

Third-party website hosting, browser, web fonts and WhatsApp may process technical information according to their respective practices. Do not share identification documents or sensitive personal information unless you understand why they are needed.

Financial planning disclaimer

Actual financing, CPF housing withdrawal limits, stamp duties, eligibility and timing vary by household and property. Financial rules may change. Confirm your circumstances directly with the appropriate authority, bank and conveyancing lawyer before making commitments. Figures may be incomplete or unsuitable for special circumstances.
