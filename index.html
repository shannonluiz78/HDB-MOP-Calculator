BUYER QUESTIONS • SINGAPORE

Frequently asked questions about selling and buying

When can I sell my HDB flat after MOP?

You can sell only after meeting the MOP and other HDB resale conditions. Standard and older unclassified flats typically have a 5-year MOP; Plus and Prime flats can have a 10-year MOP. Check your flat classification and HDB records. Official source

How much CPF OA can I use when buying my next home?

CPF from a completed sale and existing OA savings may be used, subject to CPF housing limits, remaining lease, valuation and retirement rules. Money refunded after age 55 may first top up your Retirement Account. Official source

How does HDB resale financing differ from a private condo?

Resale HDB bank loans generally use both 30% MSR and 55% TDSR. HDB housing loans use HDB eligibility and loan rules. Private condominiums generally use TDSR and LTV rather than MSR. Official source

How is Buyers Stamp Duty (BSD) calculated?

Singapore residential BSD applies marginal tax rates to the higher of the agreed price and market value. Your actual stamp duty depends on the transaction value and prevailing IRAS rules. Official source

Will I pay ABSD when replacing my HDB with another home?

ABSD depends on citizenship, property count at purchase and any applicable relief or remission. Selling first can change the property count; married-couple refund conditions are separate. Verify with IRAS. Official source

Can I buy a resale HDB without taking a housing loan?

Yes, eligible buyers may pay using eligible CPF savings and cash. A valid HFE letter and HDB purchase conditions still apply; cash-over-valuation must be funded in cash. Official source
